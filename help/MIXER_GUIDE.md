# Mixer and Routing Guide

## Introduction
The new Mixer interface allows you to view and adjust the volume, pan, mute, and solo status of your tracks in a vertical channel strip layout. Additionally, it offers comprehensive routing features, including Busses and Auxiliary Sends.

## Opening the Mixer
You can toggle the Mixer view by clicking the **Mixer** button on the main toolbar.

## Channel Strip Components
Each track in your project (Audio Tracks and Bus Tracks) is represented as a Channel Strip in the Mixer:
* **Track Name:** Displays the name of the track.
* **Sends:** Allows you to send a portion of this track's audio to any Bus track in the project. You can adjust the send amount using the small horizontal slider.
* **Pan:** Adjusts the stereo positioning of the track.
* **Volume Fader:** Controls the overall volume of the track before it reaches its destination.
* **M / S Buttons:** Mutes or Solos the track.
* **Output Routing:** At the bottom of the strip, a dropdown lets you choose where the output of this track goes. By default, it goes to the **Master** output. You can change this to route the entire signal to a Bus track instead.

## Busses and Master Effects
You can create a new Bus Track from the application menu (`Tracks -> Add New -> Bus Track`). Bus tracks will appear in both the main timeline and the Mixer.

The **Master Fader** appears on the right side of the Mixer and contains an effect rack, allowing you to easily add master limiting, EQ, or compression to your entire project.
